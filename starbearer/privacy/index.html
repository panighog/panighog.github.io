Privacy Policy

Star Bearer: Maze Survival · by PanigHog · Effective date: 26 November 2026

In short: Star Bearer has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and the one-time purchase "Star Bearer Pro" is handled by Google Play. Google may collect some data for those services, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Star Bearer: Maze Survival ("the game") is made by PanigHog ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at panighog@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information, and we do not run servers that receive data from the game.

The game saves the following only on your device: your floor progress, stars, best times, upgrades, settings, and whether you own Star Bearer Pro. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup; that backup is managed by Google, not by us.

To check whether your device is online, the game sometimes contacts a public website. No personal information is sent in that check.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to revive once per floor or to reset your upgrades. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar laws, the game asks for your consent before ads can use your data for personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

Star Bearer Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation from Google so it can unlock Pro, and keeps a record of it only on your device. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without ever watching an ad; ads only play when you choose to watch one.
  • You can change your ad consent in the game's Settings, and manage your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about data collected by Google can be made to Google through its privacy controls. You can still contact us with any question.

7. Security and data sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone, other than through the Google services described above, which use encrypted connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using a new service that collects data, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
